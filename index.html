Building yard

Laying out the lot…

Drag a crate to slide it. Crates with arrows run on rails and only move along that line. Hatched crates are padlocked: they stay put until every crate of the padlock's color has left the yard. When a crate sits fully inside the opening of its own color's gate, it is pulled out. Every yard is freshly generated and checked solvable; par is the fewest moves the solver found (one move is one drag). Every 5 cleared yards the next level gets bigger and trickier.
